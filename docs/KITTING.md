# Bins, one set per phase

Options on: gen2. Generated by `tools/kitting.py` from `plan/kits.yaml`; do not edit by hand.

Sort everything in Phase 0, while the printer still prints. That is also the inventory the plan asks for: a bag that comes up short shows up here, not mid-build. Removed hardware is sorted in Phase 3 into the "keep" bins, then split into the phase bins at the end of the teardown. Printed parts stay in their labelled kit bags unless a bin is listed for them.

**71 bins, 89 slots of 1x1.** Sizes are Gridfinity units (1x1 = 42 × 42 mm).
Plus two standing bins: **Kit spares** (1x2, spares) and **Not needed again** (2x2, scrap).

## Boxes (20 slots each)

Only the current phase's box is open. A box can hold phases that are not next to each other; it is simply opened again when its next phase comes. The Phase 3 bins are filled during the teardown and emptied into the phase bins at the end of it, so from then on their slots are free.

- **Box 1** (18 of 20 slots): P1 Bench A, P6 Belts & gantry
- **Box 2** (20 of 20 slots): P3 Teardown, P10 Puck holders
- **Box 3** (13 of 20 slots): P4 Bench B, P11 Dock & tools, P12 Door & finish
- **Box 4** (20 of 20 slots): P5 Z-axis, P9 Align & close
- **Box 5** (18 of 20 slots): P7 Toolhead, P8 Sensors & PTFE
